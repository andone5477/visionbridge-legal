# VisionBridge Privacy Policy

**Last updated**: 2026-09-28
**Version**: 1.2.0

A Traditional Chinese version is the canonical text and is available
inside the iOS app at Settings → Privacy → "查閱隱私政策". This English
translation is provided for reference and for Apple App Store
submission purposes.

## Scope

This policy describes how the VisionBridge iOS application
("the App") collects, processes, and uses user data. Section 13
covers the public website wisbridge.app and its inquiry form.

## 1. Data We Collect

1. **Anonymous identifier** — a UUID generated by Firebase
   Authentication.
2. **Email address** (only for users who sign in) — if a user chooses
   Sign in with Apple or email-based sign-in, the corresponding email
   is collected. Anonymous mode does not collect email.
3. **Device events** — eye-gaze-triggered phrase selections, device
   state changes, battery level changes, and inactivity alerts
   (auxiliary reminder events).
4. **Device metadata** — iOS version, model identifier, app version,
   locale.
5. **Pairing relationships** — the mapping between caregiver and
   patient devices.
6. **Anonymous telemetry** (optional) — eye-tracking stability,
   ARKit tracking quality, gaze standard deviation at the moment an
   inactivity alert fires. The user can disable telemetry in
   Settings.
7. **Crash reports** (Crashlytics) — call stacks and device
   information when the app crashes, used solely to fix software
   defects.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

- Real names, dates of birth, national ID numbers, health insurance
  information
- Residential addresses, GPS location, IP-derived location
- Contacts, photos, microphone audio content
- Health diagnostic information, medical records, vital signs
- Cross-app tracking data (Apple ATT is not invoked)

## 3. Purposes of Use

- Synchronize patient phrase selections to paired caregivers in
  real time
- App functionality (push notifications, pairing validation,
  inactivity reminders)
- App improvement (when telemetry is enabled)
- Bug diagnosis (Crashlytics)

## 4. What We Will Never Do

- Serve commercial advertisements
- Sell data to third parties
- Share data with other apps or companies

## 5. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Device event records (phraseSelected, batteryWarning, inactivityAlert) | **Automatically deleted after 30 days** |
| Pairing relationships | Deleted immediately on unpair |
| Anonymous telemetry | Deleted with event records (30 days) |
| Crash reports | 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics default) |
| Firebase Authentication UID | Deleted on account deletion |
| Email address (if applicable) | Deleted on account deletion |

Users may also clear all their data immediately via the in-app
"Delete all my data" function without waiting for automatic deletion.

## 6. Cross-Border Transfer

The App uses Google Firebase services. User-generated data
(events, pairing, telemetry) is stored in **asia-east1 (Changhua,
Taiwan)** — within Taiwan.

Crash reports (Crashlytics) are transmitted via Google's global
network and stored in de-identified form, which may involve
cross-border transfer to the United States. **Crash reports never
include personally identifying information.**

## 7. User Rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法 Article 3),
and aligned with comparable provisions in GDPR / CCPA where
applicable, users may:

- **Inspect** their event history within the app's Settings
- **Correct** their display name
- **Request a data copy** by contacting support@wisbridge.app
  (response within 30 days)
- **Delete** all their data via Settings → Danger Zone →
  "Delete all my data"
- **Withdraw consent** by disabling telemetry or deleting their
  account

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies.
The App does not use third-party trackers.
The App does not invoke Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT).

## 9. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Data region |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication | Anonymous / Apple / Email sign-in | Global (de-identified) |
| Firebase Firestore | Event records, pairing state | asia-east1 (Changhua) |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging | Cross-device push notifications | Global edge nodes |
| Firebase Cloud Functions | Pairing code validation, auxiliary reminder dispatch | asia-east1 |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reports | Global (de-identified) |
| Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) | iOS push delivery | Global edge nodes |

## 10. Minors

The App does not actively collect data from users under 13.
Patient identity is set by the caregiver; we recommend caregivers
choose an anonymous display name.

## 11. Policy Changes

When this policy changes, we will notify users via an in-app
prompt and require renewed consent.

## 12. Contact

## 13. Website (wisbridge.app) and Inquiry Form

The public website **wisbridge.app** sets no cookies, loads no
third-party scripts or fonts, and runs no analytics or advertising
trackers. The interactive demo on the site runs entirely in your
browser: it does not use the camera, does not track your eyes, and
sends nothing to any server.

The site has one form, the **institutional inquiry form**
(`POST /api/inquiry`). If you use it:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Data collected | Organization or household name, contact person, email or phone (required); service size and free-text message (optional) |
| Purpose | Replying to your inquiry and arranging a pilot or partnership discussion. Not used for marketing. |
| Storage | Google Cloud Firestore, **asia-east1 (Taiwan)**, written only by our server function; no client can read it |
| Notification copy | One email to support@wisbridge.app (Gmail; may involve cross-border transfer). Deleted by hand when the inquiry is closed. |
| Retention | **Up to 180 days** from submission, then deleted automatically by a scheduled Firestore policy (may lag a few days). You may ask us to delete it earlier at any time. |
| Logs | Our function logs a record ID and an error category only; it does not store your IP address, user agent, or form contents. Google's managed request logs are outside our application. |
| Your rights | Email support@wisbridge.app to access, copy, correct, restrict, or delete your data; we reply within 30 days. |

Please do not enter medical records, national ID numbers, or other
sensitive personal data in the form. If you do, we delete that
record as soon as the inquiry is handled.

## Medical Disclaimer

VisionBridge is an **assistive communication tool**, not a medical
device. It does not replace the judgment of medical professionals.

- The App **does not detect** seizures, arrhythmias, respiratory
  abnormalities, or any vital signs.
- The "inactivity alert" feature is an auxiliary reminder for
  caregivers — **it is not a medical alarm**.
- In any suspected medical emergency, **call 119** (Taiwan
  emergency services) or contact a medical professional
  immediately.
